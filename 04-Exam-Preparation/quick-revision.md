## Domain 1 · Cloud concepts

- **Cloud computing:** on-demand delivery of compute, storage and related services.
- **Public cloud:** provider-operated environment; **private:** dedicated to one organisation; **hybrid:** mix of private/on-premises and public.
- **IaaS:** you manage OS/applications; **PaaS:** provider manages hosting platform; **SaaS:** ready-to-use software.
- **Scale up/down:** bigger/smaller instance; **out/in:** more/fewer instances.
- **High availability:** keep service accessible; **reliability:** recover from failure; **predictability:** estimate performance/spend.
- **CapEx:** up-front purchase; **OpEx:** ongoing costs; **Spot:** interruptible discounted capacity.

## Domain 2 · Architecture and services

- **Region:** geographical area; **zone:** physically separate datacenter group within region.
- **Management hierarchy:** management group → subscription → resource group → resource.
- **VM:** guest OS control; **VMSS:** scalable VM groups; **App Service:** web apps; **Functions:** event-driven code; **AKS:** Kubernetes orchestration.
- **VNet:** network boundary; **subnet:** portion of IP space; **VPN Gateway:** encrypted VPN; **ExpressRoute:** private provider connectivity.
- **Blob:** objects; **Files:** network shares; **Disks:** VM block storage; **Queue:** messages; **Table:** NoSQL structured entities.
- **LRS:** one location; **ZRS:** zones; **GRS:** second region; **GZRS:** zones plus second region.
- **Migrate:** workload assessment/move; **Data Box:** physically transfer bulk data.
- **Entra ID:** identity; **MFA:** multiple factors; **Conditional Access:** contextual sign-in rules; **RBAC:** permissions.
- **Zero Trust:** verify explicitly, least privilege, assume breach.
- **Key Vault:** secrets/keys/certificates; **Defender for Cloud:** security posture/workload protection.

## Domain 3 · Management and governance

- **Pricing Calculator:** estimate future spend; **Cost Management:** assess actual/forecast spend.
- **Budget:** alerts at thresholds, not an automatic stop to spending.
- **Tag:** metadata; **Policy:** rules/audits; **RBAC:** privileges; **lock:** blocks specified management changes.
- **Purview:** data governance, risk and compliance offerings.
- **ARM:** Azure management plane; **Bicep/ARM templates:** declarative deployment.
- **Portal:** GUI; **Cloud Shell:** browser shell; **CLI/PowerShell:** automation; **Arc:** outside-Azure resources.
- **Advisor:** recommendations; **Monitor:** metrics/logs/alerts; **Application Insights:** app performance.
- **Service Health:** Azure platform impacts; **Resource Health:** individual resource impacts.
